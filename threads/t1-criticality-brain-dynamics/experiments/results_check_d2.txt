CHECK A -- what d2 actually is
==============================================================================
 trial 0: sum(phi)=-19.034472  d2=6.335456  1/(sqrt(p)*DCgain)=+6.335456  1/(sqrt(p)*sqrt(S(0)/sig2))=6.335456
 trial 1: sum(phi)=+0.171392  d2=0.262029  1/(sqrt(p)*DCgain)=+0.262029  1/(sqrt(p)*sqrt(S(0)/sig2))=0.262029
 trial 2: sum(phi)=-19.938576  d2=6.621359  1/(sqrt(p)*DCgain)=+6.621359  1/(sqrt(p)*sqrt(S(0)/sig2))=6.621359
  => d2 == 1/(sqrt(p) * DC gain) == sigma_innov / (sqrt(p) * sqrt(S(0))) exactly.
  => d2 is a monotone reparametrisation of the ZERO-FREQUENCY susceptibility.
  => (1 - sum(phi)) is exactly the coefficient tested by an augmented Dickey-Fuller
     unit-root test:  dy_t = rho*y_{t-1} + sum(gamma_i dy_{t-i}) + e_t, rho = sum(phi)-1.

     critical manifold). It is NOT distance to the bifurcation set.
